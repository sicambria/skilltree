Getting Started

What is SkillTree?

SkillTree helps you map, assess, and grow your skills. Visualize your skill landscape, find learning paths, and connect with peers.

How to use this app
  • Discover — Browse all skills and career trees to see what's available.
  • My Growth — Rate your current skill levels, set goals, and track progress over time.
  • Community — Connect with peers, get recommendations, and find complementary skills.
  • Contribute — Create new skills, build skill trees, and add training resources.
Skill Levels

Skills are rated 1-5: 1 (Initial) → 2 (Basic) → 3 (Intermediate) → 4 (Advanced) → 5 (World Class). Rate yourself honestly and update as you grow.

Learning Plan

Create a 3-month, 1-year, and 3-year plan. The plan auto-classifies your growth as deepen, broaden, or pivot based on your goals.
