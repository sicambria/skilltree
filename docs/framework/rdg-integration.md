# Regenerative Developmental Goals (RDG) Integration

This project has undergone an ontological shift from traditional technical skills to a framework of **Regenerative Development**. This shift aligns the skill tree with the "Transformative Changemaking" curriculum, moving beyond mitigation toward active restoration and systemic healing.

## The RDG vs. SDG Paradigm

Unlike common sustainability frameworks (SDGs), the RDG framework integrated here emphasizes:
- **Active Regeneration**: Shifting from "doing less harm" to "actively creating net-positive impact."
- **Living Systems Intelligence**: Collaborating within the living web rather than managing it as a machine.
- **Inner Development**: Recognizing that systemic change requires concomitant internal psychological and emotional evolution.

## New Skill Domains

### 1. Inner Development (Self)
Atomic skills for building the "Inner Landscape" required for lifelong practice.
- **Key Skills**: Transformative Social Systems (TSS), Ecological Grief Processing, Prefigurative Temporality.
- **Focus**: Head, heart, and will integration; psychological resilience.

### 2. Ecological Restoration (Nature)
Skills for actively collaborating with the biosphere.
- **Key Skills**: RDG 8: Continental Corridors, Hózhó Principle, Bioregional Animism.
- **Focus**: Shifting from humanity as a threat to humanity as a conscious restorer.

### 3. Economic Democracy (Society)
Post-growth strategies for a circular and fair economy.
- **Key Skills**: RDG 15: Economic Democracy, Solidarity Economy Design, Post-Growth Business Logic.
- **Focus**: Purposeful work and fair distribution within material limits.

### 4. Systems Governance (Systems)
Advanced tools for systemic diagnosis and institutional change.
- **Key Skills**: Institutional Acupuncture, Warm Data Analysis, Polycentric Governance.
- **Focus**: Finding high-leverage intervention points in complex networks.

## Career Paths (Trees)

The following high-level trees now synthesize these skills:
- **Transformative Changemaker**: A generalist path for systemic impact.
- **Bioregional Weaver**: For those working at the intersection of local ecology and community.
- **Regenerative Economist**: For designers of new, net-positive business models.
- **Systems Healer**: Focusing on the intersection of trauma, governance, and restoration.

## Maintenance of 2075-Proof Skills

Specific technical tools (e.g., specific software or coding frameworks) have been removed in favor of foundational principles. By focusing on **Relational Ontologies** and **Systems Thinking**, this curriculum prepares users for human-specific roles that remain relevant beyond the age of AI.
